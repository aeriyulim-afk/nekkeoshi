A NOTE FROM THE WRITER

Behind the
stories.

Hi. I'm the person with too many plots and absolutely no intention of keeping them to myself.

I write stories because my mind refuses to shut up. I like complicated people, messy emotions, impossible situations, and the little moments that make fictional worlds feel real.

Some stories might emotionally damage you a little. But I do have a soft spot for happy endings. Most of the time.

WHY TWO PEN NAMES?

Nekkeoshi is home to my general AUs across fandoms and genres. bangsniirene is reserved for my darker, heavier psychological stories, including Dissonance and After the Sentence. Different shelves, different moods, same overactive imagination.
